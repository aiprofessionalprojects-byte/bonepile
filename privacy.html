Privacy PolicyBonepile: Domino Roguelike

Effective October 23, 2026.

The short version: Bonepile does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

Your game progress, settings and run history are saved only on your device. They are never sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Purchases

The optional full-game unlock is processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your name, email, payment details or Apple ID. The app only receives a confirmation that the purchase was made, so it can unlock the full game. Apple's and Google's own privacy policies cover how they handle your payment.

What we don't do

  • No sign-up or account of any kind.
  • No advertising or ad networks.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or tracking tools.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone.
  • No selling or sharing of data, because we don't have any.

Children

Bonepile collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Contacting us

If you email us for support, we use your email address only to reply to you and don't share it. Contact: bonepilegame.support@gmail.com

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the effective date above.
