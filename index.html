How do I play?

Tap tiles in your hand that match an open end of the line, then tap Play. You can play one to five tiles at once. Beat each table's target score before you run out of plays. Make the open ends add up to a multiple of 5 to score the Count. The Rules screen on the main menu explains every combo.

What's free, and what does the unlock include?

Parlors 1 and 2 are free to play as a demo. A single one-time purchase unlocks the full game: all eight parlors, more starting sets, eight Stakes, the Daily Table and every charm unlock. There are no ads and no subscriptions.

I bought the full game. How do I get it on a new device?

Sign in with the same Apple ID (or Google account on Android), open Bonepile and tap Restore purchase on the main menu or in Settings. You won't be charged again.

Where is my progress saved?

Only on your device. Bonepile has no accounts and no cloud saves, so deleting the app removes your progress. Your full-game purchase is never lost; restore it as above.

How do I start over?

Open Settings, tap Reset progress, then confirm. This clears your progress but keeps your full-game purchase.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple or Google. To ask for a refund, visit reportaproblem.apple.com on iPhone or iPad, or use Google Play's order history on Android.
